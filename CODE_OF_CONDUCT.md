# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project free from harassment, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience
level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, give actionable feedback, accept responsibility, focus on what is best for the
community, and protect any private information encountered during security work.

Harassment, discriminatory language, deliberate exposure of private information, personal
attacks, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository's security reporting channel.
Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban
contributors whose behavior is harmful. Enforcement decisions will be made in good faith and
with regard to context, impact, and safety.

This compact policy is based on the principles of Contributor Covenant 2.1.
